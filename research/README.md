# Primary research

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `survey_responses_anonymised.csv` | 8 real responses to the 2-minute wishlist survey (names and timestamps removed; the author's own test response excluded) |
| `survey_form_v2_apps_script.gs` | Script that builds the longer follow-up survey in Google Forms |
| `interview_guide.docx` | 20-minute interview guide for the planned 5-6 user interviews |

Headline results from the survey (n = 8, 6 Myntra users):
- Why they save instead of buying: waiting for a sale (3), budget not available (2), saving for an occasion (2), comparing with other items (2), unsure of size or fit (1).
- 5 of 8 bought a few or none of the items they saved in the last 3 months.
- Where they look before buying: product reviews in the app (4), YouTube or Instagram (4).
- What would make them buy (no discounts): better or honest reviews (4 of the 7 who answered).
